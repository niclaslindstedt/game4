title: Free ride, and the start card
---

FREE RIDE on the front door: the whole map with nobody else on it — no lights, no laps, no checkpoint owed — to explore the hills, the deep powder and the kickers out in the country. It opens a start card first: the map's seed (typed, stepped or dealt fresh) with a chart of it beside the rows — the hills, the woods, the loop, the grid and every kicker marked the way it throws — which you tap to start anywhere on the map; the date, from December to mid-April; the hour, sunrise to sunset on that date; and how deep the snow is, from a dusting to a bottomless dump. The HUD shows the ride's clock, its best air and the distance ridden, the minimap drops the checkpoints, and a reset stands you on the nearest point of the track.

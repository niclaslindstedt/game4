title: The campaign
---

A CAMPAIGN on the front door: three shelves of six pinned maps — the Foothills, the Timberline and the Summit, climbing from bright open hills to fog, falling snow and the night — each a race, a time trial, two races, a time trial and a race. Race rungs are ridden against the same three rivals, who never lean on you, for 3, 2 and 1 points; time trial rungs pay a bronze, silver or gold medal against times set off a competent rider's run. A podium or a medal opens the next map, and winning a shelf's table opens the next shelf. Every map's box shows its loop, its sky and start hour and what you have won on it, and the finish plate on a rung says what it paid and what it opened, with NEXT MAP to ride on. RACE and TIME TRIAL now ride these same maps, picked on a level card out of the shelves you have opened, so every record is a record round a loop anyone can ride; a free ride and a link's seed still ride any map you like.

type: Changed
title: The birds, the animals and the course's marks are drawn from models made in Blender
---

Every bird over the woods and every animal in the snow — all eleven birds and twelve animals of the wildlife — and the course's marks (the banded checkpoint stakes with their pennants and the marker over the owed gate, and the inflatable arch over the start line) are now drawn from models made in Blender off the game's own numbers, in place of the shapes the game built in code: a bird with a keeled breast, a real bill, a fanned tail and, on the raven and the eagle, fingered wingtips; an animal with a spine, jointed legs with hooves or paws, ears, eyes and its antlers or horns; a stake with a spike and a cap, a pennant that ripples, and an arch sewn in panels with its ballast and blowers at each foot. They are painted, flapped, walked and lit exactly as before — the models carry no colour or motion of their own — and the code-built ones are one switch away (`VITE_MODEL_BIRDS=0`, `VITE_MODEL_BEASTS=0`, `VITE_MODEL_GATES=0`).

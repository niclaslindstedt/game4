---
type: Changed
title: The arrows lean, and W and S lean in the air
---

On a keyboard, ↑ now leans the rider forward and ↓ leans him back, so a sled in the air tips the way the arrow points; W and S stay the throttle and the brake, and pressed while the sled is in the air they lean it forward and back too (S then leans instead of braking). A throttle held off the lip stays a throttle, so a jump taken with the gas on does not land on its nose, and an arrow held over W or S wins. A keyboard layout you never rebound moves to the new keys by itself.

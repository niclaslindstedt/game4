title: Replays, on a TV camera, in slow motion
---

Every race and time trial is recorded as the controls that rode it, and WATCH REPLAY on the finish plate or the pause card plays it back: the same race again, the rivals included, the trails in the snow cut afresh as it plays. It opens on a BROADCAST camera that cuts to the moments the run marked — a flight off a kicker, a place taken, a trunk or a rival met, a wipeout, the flag — from a lens standing at the edge of the wood before the sled gets there, and runs each of them in slow motion. C walks the other cameras; Escape leaves.

OPTIONS on the front door: the picture in eight rows you can trade one at a time — resolution, draw distance, the ground's detail, the trails in the snow, the forest, shadows, spray and smooth edges — or all at once with a preset, each applied live over the race behind the card, with a first visit picking the preset your device can hold by timing it; master, engine and effects volumes; every key rebindable on its own page; for touchscreens, the lever on either side, the thumbs' travel and an inverted lean; and how much the sled helps you, with the steering hold and the air levelling each full, half or off.

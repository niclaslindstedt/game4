type: Added
title: Five kinds of tree in ten shapes each, woods you can see into, and more wildlife
---

The woods are no longer one spruce copied: every map now grows **spruce, fir, pine, larch and birch** in the mix its country grows (a northern forest is mostly spruce and pine, the high alpine fir and larch, the tundra stunted spruce and mountain birch), and every kind comes in **ten shapes** — a dense spire, a narrow pencil, a snow ghost bowed under its load, a stand tree bare underneath, a storm-broken top, a pine's flat crown on its tall bare trunk, a larch's bare winter skeleton, a birch with two or three stems off one root, and more. On a free ride, a link's own seed and every new map, the woods now **bunch into clumps** with open ground between and have **lanes** winding through them, so you see well into a wood and there is always a way between two groups of trees; the campaign's maps keep the woods they were curated with. And more lives in them: **red squirrels, roe deer, a wolverine and a wolf pack** in the boreal forest, **black grouse** and **snow buntings** that burst off the snow as you pass, a **black woodpecker** and a **great grey owl** — and in the open country the **arctic fox**, **chamois** on the alpine faces and **musk oxen** on the tundra. OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ FOREST also sets how many tree shapes are drawn, so a slower device keeps its frame rate.

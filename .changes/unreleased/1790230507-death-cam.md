title: A death cam for a wipeout
---

Come off the sled and the camera goes with you: it leaves your chosen view, follows the rider as he is thrown and closes in on him, the picture easing into slow motion just before he hits the snow. Once he stops moving it tilts down onto him and rises into the sky, swaying, until you are stood back up on the track. The rider now lies still for a moment before the reset to give the shot its beat; pressing reset still skips it.

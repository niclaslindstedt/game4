type: Added
title: Modelled trees
---

Every tree in the woods — all twenty kinds in each of their ten variants — is now drawn from a model made in Blender off the same numbers the forest was built from: a spruce's boughs droop from a dark crown with the snow lying along them, pines carry lobed needle pads on real branches, larches hang their twigs, and the bare broadleaves fork into fine crowns of twigs with the rowan's berries and the beech's kept leaves on them. Each country still paints its own trees and loads them with its own snow. The code-built trees are one switch away: `VITE_MODEL_TREES=0`, or `make ci-models MODELS=off` for every CI build.

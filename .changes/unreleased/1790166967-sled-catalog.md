title: Four sleds, and drifts across the track
---

The field now rides four different machines — a short-tread trail sled that is quickest on the groomer, the all-round crossover, a long-tread mountain sled that floats through fresh snow and pushes wide on packed bends, and a light cross sled that takes the hardest landings — and every map now has stretches of its track drifted over with fresh snow, some maps barely any and some nearly half the loop.

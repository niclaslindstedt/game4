title: Four sleds, more power, drifts across the track, and a grid on it
---

The field now rides four different machines — a short-tread trail sled that is quickest on the groomer, the all-round crossover, a long-tread mountain sled that floats through fresh snow and pushes wide on packed bends, and a light cross sled geared short that takes the hardest landings — all of them on 850-class power, with top speeds of 145–170 km/h. Every map now has stretches of its track drifted over with fresh snow, some barely any and some nearly half the loop; the race starts from a grid on the track behind the start line; and the woods leave room to ride between any two trees. On a touchscreen the throttle lever is wide open the moment your thumb lands: slide up to ease off, and further up to brake.

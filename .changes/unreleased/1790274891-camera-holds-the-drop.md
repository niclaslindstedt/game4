type: Fixed
title: The chase cameras keep the rider in the picture off a big jump or a cliff
---

On the **chase**, **far** and **high** cameras, a long flight or a drop off a cliff no longer loses the rider out of the bottom of the screen. The camera still hangs back as the sled takes off and falls, which is what makes a jump feel like air, but it now trails by only a few metres at most, and it **tilts** to follow the rider down (or up) so the sled stays in frame all the way to the landing. On ordinary snow the framing is unchanged.

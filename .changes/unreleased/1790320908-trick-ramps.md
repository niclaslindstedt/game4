---
type: Changed
title: Trick mode's ramps, in three sizes with landing slopes
---

A tricks run's track now carries far more ramps (ten to eighteen down a loop, where it had four to twelve), in three sizes: a low kicker onto snow falling away, and medium and high lips with a flat deck and a landing slope dug down past it, so a big jump taken at the right speed comes down on the slope instead of slamming onto the flat. The country around the loop is the race map's own, trees and all.

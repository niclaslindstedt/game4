title: Every tree casts its shadow, and none of them pops in
---

Tree shadows no longer switch on as you ride up to a wood, stop at a hard line across the snow, or vanish when the camera turns away from the tree behind you. Every tree whose shadow can reach the snow round you now casts it — near or far, in front of you or behind — and the shadows fade out gently at the edge of their reach instead of ending in a line. The shadow follows the view ahead of the camera rather than the sled, so it covers the snow you are looking at. OPTIONS ▸ SHADOWS is now three modes: OFF, SLEDS (the machines and their riders alone, drawn sharp, for almost no cost) and ALL (every tree's too — each drawn from a lighter stand-in unless FOREST is on HIGH).

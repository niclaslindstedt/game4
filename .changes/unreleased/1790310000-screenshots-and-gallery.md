title: Screenshots and the gallery
---

Press **Enter** mid-race (or take a screenshot with the phone's own buttons) and the game photographs the moment — the snow, the sled, the spray and the HUD over them, signed in the corner with the game's mark and captioned with the map's seed, the lap, the speed and the sled. **H** takes the readouts down for a clean frame of the snow alone (also OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ HUD). The last forty pictures are kept on the device under the front door's new **GALLERY**: flip through them, and **share** them (the phone's own share sheet), **copy**, **save** or delete them. The desktop app's View ▸ Take Screenshot presses the same shutter, and in the store app a screenshot taken with the phone's own buttons is filed in the gallery too.

title: A tricks mode, and tricks to score in it
---

TRICKS on the front door: two minutes alone on the map with a field of groomed kickers laid along its track — small, medium and large in turn — scored for everything you do in the air. Carry the lean all the way back off a lip for a backflip, all the way forward for a front flip, or throw the bars all the way over for a 360, tapping again for more; hold the trick button (F or X, or the TRICK press on a touchscreen) for a one-footer, a can-can or a tuck — and let go before you land. The air pays by the second and by the metre, every trick raises the multiplier, and a combo strung across linked jumps is banked when you ride away clean: land it hard and you keep the points but not the multiplier, get thrown or land still in a pose and it is gone. The combo reads over the nose as you build it, with your score and the seconds left in the top corner.

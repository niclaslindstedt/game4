title: Weather, night and the headlamp
---

Every map is now dealt one of six skies — clear, fair-weather cumulus, a sheet of high cloud, the flat light of an overcast where the bumps are hard to read, falling snow from a few flakes to a blizzard with spindrift blowing off the crests, or a valley fog — and a quarter of the races start around sunset and ride into the night under the moon and the stars, every sled's headlamp throwing a pool of light on the snow and its taillight glowing red ahead of you. The weather never changes the map itself: the same seed is the same ground under any sky.

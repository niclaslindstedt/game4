title: Simpler free ride start card
---

The free ride's start card asks in words instead of faders: SEASON (early winter, midwinter, late winter, spring), TIME of day (morning, day, evening or night, which now rides in the dark under the moon and the stars) and SNOW (thin 10 cm, medium 26 cm, thick 38 cm, very thick 50 cm). Season and time start on the map's own day until you change them.

title: Six new sleds, sharper handling, a new rider and liveries
---

The sleds are rebuilt as six real classes of snowmobile traced off photographs — the all-round Fox, the trail Hare, the mountain Ibex, the snocross Stoat, the touring Bison and the work-sled Beaver — each in four liveries with its suspension on show, steering far sharper with the throttle and brake biting through a corner as on a real sled, and ridden by a new rider who stands on the pegs, hangs off into turns and folds into his knees on a landing.

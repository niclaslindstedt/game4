Powder Run exists: a snowmobile racing game in the browser — one generated map of snowy hills, mountain flanks, open powder fields and snow-loaded conifer forests, a packed-snow race loop laid over it, one sled with its rider, a three-lap race against three bot rivals from a start out in the deep powder, trails that stay in the snow behind every sled, a chase camera, a HUD with the speed, the lap and the checkpoints, the race clock and your place, keyboard and touch controls, synthesized engine and snow sound, and installable as an offline-capable PWA.

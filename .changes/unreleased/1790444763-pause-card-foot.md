---
type: Changed
title: The pause card is shorter, and fits a phone held sideways
---

**RESUME** and **OPTIONS** stay as the card's two rows. **RESTART**, **REPLAY** and **MAIN MENU**, the three presses that end the race, now sit side by side in one quiet strip along the card's foot, each a small mark over a short word. The card is about a fifth shorter, so on a phone held sideways every press is on screen without scrolling. A stray thumb is also less likely to hit a press that throws the race away.

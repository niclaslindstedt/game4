---
type: Changed
title: A fuller pause card, and the corner's presses under the minimap
---

The pause card now bills the race so far — your place, the clock, the record you are riding against, your best air and the distance ridden — and has an OPTIONS panel with the camera and the sound (on or off, and the master, engine and effects faders), applied to the held frame at once. SOUND moved from the card onto that panel. In the HUD, the pause, reset and camera presses now sit under the minimap instead of above it.

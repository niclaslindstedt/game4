title: PRESET ▸ AUTO fits every picture row to your machine for sixty frames a second
---

OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ **PRESET** has a new first stop, **AUTO**, which is where a fresh visit starts. While it is on, the game times itself drawing the race behind the front door for a few seconds and sets **each row on its own**, not a whole preset, for sixty frames a second. The rows that cost the most for what they show go first, and the ones you would notice (sharpness, the furrows in the snow, the trees' shadows) are kept longest. A fast machine gets everything, up to DISTANCE MAX. It checks again on every visit and whenever AUTO is pressed. Moving any row yourself turns it off and keeps your picture.

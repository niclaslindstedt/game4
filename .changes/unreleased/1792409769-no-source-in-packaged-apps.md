type: Changed
title: The phone and desktop apps no longer link to the source, and every build links its privacy policy
---

The version in the menu and the build label on the HUD name the build as plain text in the phone and desktop apps, which leave out the website's privacy and support pages. A PRIVACY link in the front door's foot strip opens the game's privacy policy on apps.agilator.se in every build, and the desktop Help menu and About panel open the game's page there too. The website keeps its links.

---
type: Changed
title: Trick flips and 360s wind up and build instead of snapping to a fixed speed
---

In a TRICKS run a flip or a 360 no longer jumps to full speed the moment the lean or the bars reach the top. The sled now gathers into the rotation, surges and settles, the way a rider's thrown weight takes the machine round. Hold the throw and it keeps building. Hold the bars over and a 360 keeps speeding up. Let go and the spin coasts down. Tapping still adds a stroke a tap, out of the same budget per flight.

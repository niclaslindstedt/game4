title: Time trial, the record book and the ghost
---

A TIME TRIAL tile on the front door: the map the menu is standing over, ridden alone under the lights for three laps or one (the chip along the foot picks which). Every finished race and trial is written into a record book kept per map, sled and length, with the day it was set; the tile shows the best for the sled you have picked, the HUD's split gains a VS BEST chip saying how far ahead or behind the record you are at each checkpoint, and the finish plate says NEW RECORD or how far off it you were. The trial that set the record is kept as the controls that rode it, and the next trial on that map is ridden beside its ghost — a pale, see-through sled on exactly the record's line, leaving no trail in the snow.

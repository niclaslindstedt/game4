title: Wipeouts, getting stuck in powder, and damage
---

Hit a tree hard, land on the nose or roll the sled at speed and the rider now comes off: he tumbles through the snow in a burst of powder, leaving a gouge where he slid, and a couple of seconds later the two of you are stood back on the track. Sit in deep powder with the belt spinning and the sled digs itself in until it is resting on its belly — ease off and rock it out with the lean and the bars, or it only digs deeper. And a new DAMAGE switch under OPTIONS ▸ ASSIST (off unless you turn it on) lets a trunk bend a ski, which pulls the sled toward its side, and hard landings wear the suspension soft, with an instrument beside the speed showing what is bent.

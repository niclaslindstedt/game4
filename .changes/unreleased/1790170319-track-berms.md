title: Plowed berms along the track
---

The race track is now lined on both sides by the snow the groomer's plough pushed off it: a knee-high windrow of chunky, freshly turned snow that wanders a little in height along the loop. The track's edge now shows as a white ridge with a shadowed side, so the line through a bend reads from well down the course. The berms are real ground: you can ride over one to reach a kicker off the track, but you feel it.

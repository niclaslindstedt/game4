type: Added
title: Cloud save in the iOS app
---

The iOS app now carries your **record book**, your **ghosts**, your **campaign** board and your preferences between your own devices through iCloud — ride a time trial on the phone and its record and its ghost are waiting on the tablet. Nothing is overwritten: each best time, each faster ghost and each better campaign result is kept from whichever device set it, and the settings you changed last win. The picture settings and the touch controls stay with the device they were set on. There is no account to make — the save follows the Apple ID the device is already signed into, and signed out of iCloud the game keeps everything on the device as before. The web game is unchanged.

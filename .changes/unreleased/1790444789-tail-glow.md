title: The taillight glows on the lens you can see, not buried behind the snow flap
---

From behind, a sled's red taillight glow was hung inside the tunnel, so the snow flap hid it and only a blocky red smear showed round its edges. The glow now sits on the lens across the tail, in front of the flap, and is drawn smooth rather than in squares (the headlamps' glow too). The red the tail lends the snow cloud behind a sled now comes from that same lens.

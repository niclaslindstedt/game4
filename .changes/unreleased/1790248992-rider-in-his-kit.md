type: Changed
title: The rider looks like a person in a jacket and pants, not a stick figure
---

The man on every sled has been rebuilt after photographs of sleds ridden from behind. He wears a **wind jacket** that is one broad mass from the hem to the shoulders, with the shoulders built in (no ball joints), its second colour over the yoke and the hem, gathered across the belly and hanging in folds down the back. His **sleeves** are baggy, creased in the crook of the elbow and bunched into flared gauntlets. His **insulated pants** have a real seat, two lobes and not a can, with baggy thighs, pads over the knees, creases behind them and gaiters over big boots. The **helmet** is a proper motocross helmet: bigger, sat down on a tall collar with no neck showing, the goggles set in the eye port with a thick frame round the lens, a chunky chin bar with a vent, a peak on the brow, a stripe over the crown and the goggle strap round the back. He also rides more like a person: he sits closer to the bars with his arms bent, his elbows out and down rather than winged up, his shoulders turning a little with the bars and his head looking into the turn.

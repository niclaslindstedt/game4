type: Changed
title: A thrown rider falls like a body — a ragdoll that the snow drags flat and stops
---

When a crash throws the rider off, he is now a **ragdoll**: jointed at the hips, shoulders, knees and elbows, his arms and legs flung loose as he goes over the bars. He no longer rolls on and on like a wheel. He goes over about once, the snow catches his arms and legs, and he slides to rest lying flat. In **powder** the snow stops him within a few metres, and the deeper it is, the sooner. At the deepest snow he ends up half buried in the body-shaped hollow he pressed into it.

title: The developer page and the benchmark
---

A DEVELOPER page, let out by holding the front door's title for seven seconds: instruments over the picture — the frame rate, what each frame cost (the engine's steps and the draw cut into its stretches, the draw calls and triangles), every probe's load, sink and travel, the trail maps in the corner, the engine's debug log and a free camera flown on I, J, K, L, U and O — with a REPRO line that turns the race on screen into a link the game stands back up (`?start=race&seed=…&t=…&pose=…`). UNLOCKS opens or shuts the campaign's shelves by hand. BENCHMARK races a pinned map through the woods and over a kicker with the whole field, drawn as fast as the machine can, scores it (100 is real time), keeps the last twenty runs, and COPY DEBUG REPORT puts the whole breakdown on the clipboard; `make bench` runs it headlessly.

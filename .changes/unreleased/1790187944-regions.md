title: Regions — four kinds of snow country
---

The same map can now be raised in four kinds of snow country: the boreal forest (every map just as before), the high alpine (steep rough country and deep bowls under taller peaks, woods only in the hollows, wind crust on the crests and rock through the steep faces), the tundra plateau (flat and open, a wind crust carved into sastrugi, the far north's late-winter light) and the birch valley (pale birches and a frozen river across the basin — a fast road of slippery ice). Each has its own look and its own colour grade over the whole picture. Pick one with the COUNTRY row on the free ride's start card, or a `?region=` link.

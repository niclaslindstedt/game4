type: Changed
title: DISTANCE draws much less on the lower stops, behind a mist, and gains a MAX stop
---

OPTIONS ▸ PICTURE ▸ **DISTANCE** now decides how far out anything is drawn, not only the trees. **LOW** draws only what it takes to ride — the course and the woods beside it, a few seconds ahead at speed — **MEDIUM** about six hundred metres and **HIGH** about thirteen hundred, each closing a **mist** over the country before its edge so the cut-off is never seen, and the ground itself is built only as far out as the stop draws, so a weaker machine has far less to draw every frame. A new fourth stop, **MAX**, draws the whole basin to the mountains on the rim with no mist at all, as HIGH used to. A picture saved on an older build keeps the stop it was on.

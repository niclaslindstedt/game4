---
type: Changed
title: Landings stick, and a jump flies with weight
---

A sled no longer **bucks** off a landing. Before, a kicker taken at speed bottomed the suspension and the springs threw the whole machine back into the air, a quarter of a metre clear for nearly half a second, so every big jump landed twice. Now every machine's shocks have the rebound damping a sled set up for jumps runs, the bump stops swallow a slam instead of handing it back, and the last part of each stroke stiffens the way a sled's shocks do for landings: the sled squats, comes back up and stays on the snow. **In the air the sled is pulled down harder** than the ground ever holds it, as the arcade racers do, so a jump is a quick, heavy arc instead of a slow-motion hang. The same kicker at race speed now flies about 1.6 s and 46 m, where it hung 2.3 s and 67 m, and you still leave the snow over exactly the crests you did before. With the lean left alone the rider now **looks at his landing** and brings the nose to the slope he is coming down on. The TRICKS run keeps its longer hang, so every flip and spin still comes round.

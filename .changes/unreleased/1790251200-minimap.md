title: A minimap
---

A round map in the top-right corner, turned so that ahead is always up: the hills shaded with their contours, the forest, the track, every checkpoint with the one you owe in red (and an arrow on the rim pointing to it when it is off the map), and the other three sleds in their colours. It zooms out as you speed up and back in when you slow down.

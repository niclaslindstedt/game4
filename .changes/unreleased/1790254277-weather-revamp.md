---
type: Changed
title: Bright skies most days, a snow system from flurries to a storm, and starry nights
---

**The weather is reworked.** A map is now dealt a **sun in the sky nearly three days in four** (clear, fair cumulus, high cloud, or **flurries**, a few flakes glinting out of a sunny sky), and a grey lid is the exception. Clear and fair days have a deeper blue sky. An **overcast** is a textured deck darker than the snow, with the sun a pale disc behind it and soft shadows, so the snow no longer reads as one white sheet. Snow now comes in three strengths: **flurries**; a **steady fall** under a grey lid; and a **storm** under black cloud, in a gale, that closes the view to a few tens of metres and puts the lamps on. The fall comes and goes in squalls, and **new snow builds up as you ride** at a real fall's rate. A storm lays a hand's depth in an hour, so a free ride left running in one ends on a buried groomer and in deeper powder: the corduroy goes, the furrows fill in, and the track rides soft. A race is too short to feel it.

**The sun no longer moves during a ride.** A map is ridden in the light it was dealt from the green to the flag. **Clear nights are full of stars**: two fields of them, each drawn a pixel or two across on every screen and twinkling low in the sky, and in a dark sky away from the moon the Milky Way rises out of the rim.

**The campaign's skies are hand-picked**: sunny days, flurries, a first snow in the foothills, a storm on the summit's Whiteout and a starry night in the Night Woods. The third foothills rung is now **Snow Pines**. The maps and their loops are unchanged.

title: Speed feels like speed — the camera stretches, surges and buzzes, and the snow and the air stream past
---

A sled at 100 km/h used to read like one at half that. The chase camera now **widens its view with pace while pulling in behind the sled**, so the machine keeps its size and the world rushes out past the edges of the frame, instead of the sled shrinking into the distance. The camera **falls behind a sled pulling away and swings in over one braking**, and past about 40 km/h it **buzzes** — harder on the groomer than in powder, and going quiet the moment the sled leaves the snow. The hood and bars cameras widen and buzz with pace too. Close to the lens, **fine ice crystals hang in clear air** and whip past at speed, and the snow has **a fine grain of patches** close in, so the ground itself streams by under the skis.

title: A real start/finish line
---

The start/finish line is now what a snowmobile race puts up: an inflatable arch in the organiser's red over the track, weighted at the feet, with its blowers and guy lines, a START · FINISH banner across the span that fits its lettering, and the line itself dyed checkered across the snow underneath. The old checkered plank on two posts — whose lettering spilled out of its box — is gone.

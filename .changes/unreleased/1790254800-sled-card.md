title: The sled card — choose your machine before the race
---

RACE now opens a sled card before the grid: the machine itself turning on a stand of snow, an arrow either side of it to step through the trail sled, the crossover, the mountain sled and the cross sled, with its top speed, 0–100 and power beside it and five bars — acceleration, top speed, cornering, powder, landings — saying where it stands against the other three. RIDE takes it onto the snow, and the game remembers the pick. Every sled in the race is now drawn as its own machine: the mountain sled's long tail and tall paddles, the trail sled's stubby tunnel, the cross sled standing taller on its long travel.

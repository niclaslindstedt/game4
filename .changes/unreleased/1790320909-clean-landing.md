title: Clean and perfect landings score in trick mode
---

A landing on a tricks run is judged by how hard the sled met the snow against what its suspension can take: a soft one is a CLEAN LANDING, a barely-there one a PERFECT LANDING, each worth points of its own the softer it was, and beside a trick in the same jump a step more of multiplier (two for a perfect one).

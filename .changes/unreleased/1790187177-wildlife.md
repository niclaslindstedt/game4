title: Birds over the forest and animals in the snow
---

The basin is alive: ravens croaking over the woods, crossbills chattering in the spruce tops, a golden eagle soaring over the ridge, coveys of white ptarmigan — and now and then a capercaillie — that burst off the snow in a whirr when a sled comes close, and in March lines of swans and skeins of geese going north overhead. On the ground, mountain hares at the wood's edge, foxes trotting the meadows, reindeer herds digging for lichen that trot off when they hear an engine, and, rarely, a moose or a lynx — each leaving its own prints in the snow, the same snow the sleds cut. Every map deals its own, the same every time you ride it, and the birds go quiet at night.

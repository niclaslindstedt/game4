title: Build a helmet from a MEASURED photograph, hollow and worn tipped — never by eye
date: 2026-09-24
scope: pwa/src/game/rider-helmet.ts, pwa/src/tools/sled-harness.ts
concepts: [helmet, face, goggles, chin-bar, peak]

Five rounds of tuning a helmet by eye went nowhere (a sticker, a jowl, a
duck bill, a gas mask); one round against a gridded photograph of a real
one fixed it. What the photograph showed that memory did not: the shell is
FLAT at the eyes (the brow 11.5 cm ahead of the head's middle, the goggles
nearly flush in a port 11 cm tall), the chin bar is the shell's own long
jaw reaching 21 cm forward with its bottom in line with the rim, and the
peak rides the CROWN level rather than sitting on the brow. A ball with
parts stuck on reads as a mask however it is tuned. Build the shell as one
surface whose reach from the head's middle follows measured profiles, cut
the port out of it and show a liner through it, put the goggles ON a face
inside, and tip the whole thing nose-down as worn — a helmet photographed
resting on its rim leans its goggles back about 10°. Wind the shell's cells
outward (check one cross product), share the seam's vertices and set the
pole's normal, or the crown shows a star and the back a seam.

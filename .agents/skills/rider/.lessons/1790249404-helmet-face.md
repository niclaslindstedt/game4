---
title: A helmet reads only when it is HOLLOW — a shell with the port cut out over a face, the goggles on the eyes inside it — judged on `--sheet=head`
date: 2026-09-24
scope: pwa/src/game/rider-helmet.ts, pwa/src/tools/sled-harness.ts
concepts: [helmet, face, goggles, chin-bar, peak]
---

A solid ball with goggles, a chin and a peak stuck on it never reads as a
helmet however the pieces are tuned — the owner said as much after three
rounds of it. What reads is the hollow: cut the port out of the shell's
grid, show a dark liner and a rim through it, put a face in there and the
goggles ON the face (open partial cylinders round it), the strap round the
outside of the back. The shell's cells must be wound outward (check one
triangle's cross product; a flipped skin turns inside out through the
port). A squashed
sphere under the port reads as a jowl, a long flat taper as a duck bill and
a thin tipped one as a bird's beak: the chin bar wants a broad front face
(half the root's width at the tip) tipped down about 0.3 rad, so its top
runs from under the goggles to the point. A peak raked past about 0.2 rad,
or set high on the crown, shows as horns from the chase camera. Look at
every angle at once on the head sheet; one view always hides the fault.
